Equine care you can trust

Professional equine veterinary services for horses at every stage of life.

Based in Owning, Co. Kilkenny, Ravaro Equine Veterinary Services provides professional veterinary care to training yards, breaking yards, stud farms and recreational horse owners throughout Kilkenny and the surrounding areas.

From routine healthcare and vaccinations to reproduction, stud medicine, lameness investigations, pre-purchase examinations and diagnostic services, our team provides knowledgeable, practical and compassionate veterinary care with the welfare of every horse at the heart of what we do.

At Ravaro Equine Veterinary Services, we understand that every horse is different.

From professional competition and training horses to young horses, breeding stock and recreational horses, each has individual healthcare requirements. Our approach is centred around providing professional, reliable and compassionate veterinary care tailored to the needs of each horse.

Based in Owning, Co. Kilkenny, we work with horse owners, trainers, breeders, stud farms and yards throughout Kilkenny and the surrounding areas.

Our aim is simple: to provide a veterinary service that clients can trust and rely on, while keeping the health, welfare and individual needs of every horse at the centre of our care.

Whether you require routine preventative healthcare, reproductive services, investigation of lameness, diagnostic imaging, laboratory work or a pre-purchase examination, our team is here to provide professional equine veterinary support.

Services

Reproduction & Stud Medicine

We provide veterinary support to breeders and stud farms throughout the breeding season.

Our reproduction and stud medicine services are focused on the individual needs of each mare, stallion and breeding programme, providing professional veterinary oversight and practical advice throughout the process.

We understand the importance of careful planning, close monitoring and timely veterinary intervention when it comes to equine reproduction.

Routine Healthcare

Regular veterinary care is an important part of maintaining your horse's health and wellbeing.

We provide routine healthcare tailored to the individual horse, helping owners and yards identify potential health concerns early and maintain horses in good health.

We work with both professional yards and private horse owners to provide practical veterinary care that supports the long-term health and wellbeing of each horse.

Lameness Investigations

Lameness can have a significant impact on a horse's comfort, welfare and performance.

Our veterinary team takes a thorough and methodical approach to lameness investigations, working to identify the source of discomfort and establish an appropriate plan for further management, treatment or investigation.

Each case is considered individually, taking into account the horse's history, clinical signs, workload and intended use.

Diagnostic Imaging & X-Ray

Diagnostic imaging can provide valuable information when investigating injury, lameness and other health concerns. We provide X-ray services as part of our diagnostic investigations, helping our veterinary team assess areas of concern and reach an informed diagnosis.

Where appropriate, diagnostic imaging may be used alongside clinical examination and other investigations to build a clearer picture of the horse's condition.

Laboratory Services

Laboratory testing can play an important role in diagnosing and monitoring a wide range of equine health conditions.

Where appropriate, we can arrange and utilise laboratory testing and diagnostic analysis to support clinical investigations, treatment decisions and ongoing monitoring of your horse's health.

Laboratory investigations may be used alongside a physical examination and other diagnostic procedures to help provide a comprehensive assessment.

Pre-Purchase Examinations

Buying a horse is a significant decision. A pre-purchase examination can provide valuable information about a horse's current health and suitability for its intended use.

We carry out pre-purchase examinations for horses intended for a variety of disciplines and purposes, helping prospective owners make a more informed decision before purchasing.

Whether you are buying a competition horse, leisure horse, young horse or horse for breeding, we can assess the horse with its intended use in mind.

Equine Vaccinations

Vaccination forms an important part of preventative healthcare.

We provide equine vaccination services and can advise owners and yards on appropriate vaccination programmes based on each horse's individual circumstances, lifestyle and level of exposure.

Regular vaccination is an important part of protecting individual horses and supporting the wider health of the equine population.

Castration

We provide veterinary castration services, with the safety and welfare of the horse our priority.

We can discuss the procedure with owners beforehand, including the most appropriate approach for the individual horse, and provide guidance on aftercare and recovery.

The Team
